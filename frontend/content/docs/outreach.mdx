## Generate pitches

1. In your results, select the creators you want to contact.
2. Choose **Outreach** and pick a collaboration type:

| Type | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Sponsored video** | A paid segment inside one of their regular videos |
| **Dedicated review** | A whole video about your product |
| **Affiliate & performance** | No upfront fee; the creator earns a commission per sale through a code or link |
| **Product gifting / seeding** | You send the product free; the creator reviews it honestly |

3. Optionally add deliverables (for example "1× 60s integration"). These are kept for **your** deal setup and are **not** put in the first message.
4. Generate. Pitches appear live as each one finishes.

## What a ReachAI pitch looks like

Every pitch is checked before you see it. Pitches:

- open by referencing the creator's real content and niche;
- briefly say what your product does for **their** audience;
- end with a low-pressure invitation to collaborate;
- are written only for channels the creator is reachable on (email, Instagram, X, LinkedIn).

Pitches **never** include clichés like "Hope this email finds you well", internal scores or prices, package details, or more than two questions. If a pitch breaks a rule, it's automatically rewritten once.

## Send from your Gmail

Open a creator's pitch. If you've [connected Gmail](/docs/gmail), check the **To**, **Subject** and **Message** (edit anything), then press **Send from you@gmail.com**. It goes from your own address, and the creator moves to **Contacted** in [Deals](/docs/deals). Their reply comes back into that deal by itself.

Not connected? The same box offers **Connect Gmail**, or **Open in your mail app**.

## Export

Or export all pitches:

| Export | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Instantly** | Cold email campaigns in Instantly |
| **Smartlead** | Email sequences in Smartlead |
| **Generic CSV / Excel** | Everything else: all channels, pitch angles and creator handles |
