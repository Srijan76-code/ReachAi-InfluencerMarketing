---
title: Get started
description: Run your first campaign in about 5 minutes.
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create a campaign

Sign in and press **New campaign** in the top bar (it's there on every page). When you run the search, the campaign is named from your brief, for example *DataLaunch · Sales · India*. Rename or delete it from the **…** menu on its card. Each card shows how many creators were found and when it was last updated. Drafts you never ran disappear from the list after a day.

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in the brief

| Section | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Brand** | Name, industry, business model (B2B, B2C, D2C), price range, and the outcome your product delivers |
| **Audience** | Who buys, their country, the creator size you want, and your competitors (optional) |
| **Campaign** | Goal, creator type (expert, mentor or peer), budget, number of creators, and platforms (leave unticked to let ReachAI choose) |

Brand name, product, audience and location are required. Creator languages follow the country (for example English and Hindi for India). Tips: [Writing a good brief](/docs/writing-a-good-brief).

</Step>
<Step>

### Find creators

Select **Find Creators**. A search takes about **10–30 seconds** and runs in the background, so you can leave the page. You'll see each step and how many creators have been scored so far.

</Step>
<Step>

### Review and save

Creators are ranked by fit and health. Open one to see their numbers, fair price and why they fit, and **save** the ones you like. See [Understanding your results](/docs/understanding-results).

</Step>
<Step>

### Write the pitches

Select creators and choose **Start outreach**. Each gets a chat in the **Outreach** tab with a personal first message drafted for you to check and send. See [Outreach](/docs/outreach).

</Step>
</Steps>
