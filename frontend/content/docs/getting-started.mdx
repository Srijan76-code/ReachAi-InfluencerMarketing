title: Getting started
description: Run your first campaign from brief to pitches.
---

## 1. Create a campaign

Sign in and select **Campaigns**, then **New campaign**.

## 2. Fill in the brief

The brief is what ReachAI uses to judge fit, so it matters most. You'll be asked for:

| Section | Fields |
|---|---|
| **Brand** | Name, website, industry, business model (B2B, B2C, D2C), product price range (low under $50, mid $50–500, high $500+), and the outcome your product delivers |
| **Audience** | Who your customers are, their locations and languages, and the creator size you want (subscriber range) |
| **Campaign** | Goal (brand awareness, leads/signups, or sales), creator type (expert, mentor, or peer/relatable), total budget, and how many creators you want |

See [Writing a good brief](/docs/writing-a-good-brief) for tips.

## 3. Run it

Select **Submit**. The run happens in the background and usually takes **1–2 minutes**. You'll see each step as it happens: understanding the brief, searching, filtering, measuring, ranking and writing the reasons. You can leave the page and come back.

## 4. Review your creators

Open the results to see the ranked list. Select a creator to see their numbers, fair price and why they fit. **Save** the ones you like. See [Understanding your results](/docs/understanding-results).

## 5. Write the pitches

Select creators and choose **Outreach**. Pick the type of collaboration, and ReachAI writes a personal first message for each creator on the channels they're reachable on. See [Outreach](/docs/outreach).
