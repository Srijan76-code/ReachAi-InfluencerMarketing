## 1. Create a campaign

Sign in and select **Campaigns**, then **New campaign**.

## 2. Fill in the brief

The brief is what ReachAI uses to judge fit, so it matters most. You'll be asked for:

| Section | Fields |
|---|---|
| **Brand** | Name, industry, business model (B2B, B2C, D2C), product price range (low under $50, mid $50–500, high $500+), and the outcome your product delivers |
| **Audience** | Who your customers are, their country, and the creator size you want (subscriber range). Creator languages follow the country: English, plus the main local language where many creators don't post in English (for example Hindi for India) |
| **Campaign** | Goal (brand awareness, leads/signups, or sales), creator type (expert, mentor, or peer/relatable), total budget, how many creators you want, and platforms (leave them unticked to let ReachAI choose; YouTube today, Instagram, X and LinkedIn coming soon) |

Brand name, what you sell, target audience and audience location are required.

See [Writing a good brief](/docs/writing-a-good-brief) for tips.

## 3. Run it

Select **Find Creators**. The run happens in the background and usually takes **10–30 seconds**. You'll see each step as it happens (understanding the brief, searching, measuring, judging, ranking and writing the reasons) and how many creators have been scored so far. You can leave the page and come back.

## 4. Review your creators

Open the results to see the ranked list. Select a creator to see their numbers, fair price and why they fit. **Save** the ones you like. See [Understanding your results](/docs/understanding-results).

## 5. Write the pitches

Select creators and choose **Outreach**. Pick the type of collaboration, and ReachAI writes a personal first message for each creator on the channels they're reachable on. See [Outreach](/docs/outreach).
