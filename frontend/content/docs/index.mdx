ReachAI helps startup and solo founders run creator campaigns without a marketing team. You describe your product and audience once. ReachAI searches for creators, checks they're real and active, ranks them by how well they fit **your** product, estimates a fair price for each one, and writes a personal first message.

## What you get from a campaign

- **A ranked list of creators** that fit your product, each with a one-line reason.
- **Real performance numbers:** typical views, reliability, engagement and expected clicks per video.
- **A fair price** for each creator, so you know when a quote is too high.
- **Risk checks:** inactive channels and suspicious engagement are filtered out before you see them.
- **Personal pitches** for email and social, ready to copy or export.
