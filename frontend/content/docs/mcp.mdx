---
title: Use ReachAI from your agent
description: Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor or any MCP agent to ReachAI and work with your campaigns and creators from there.
---

ReachAI runs an [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) server. Once your agent is connected, you can ask it things like "which of my campaigns have finished?" or "show me the top 10 creators for the DataLaunch campaign" without opening the app. Anything your agent saves shows up in ReachAI straight away, because it is the same account and the same data.

## Connect

Open [Agents](/agents) and click **Onboard your agent**. It copies one line:

```text
Set up ReachAI for me. Fetch https://reach-ai-influencer-marketing.vercel.app/llms.txt and follow it.
```

Paste it into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code or any other agent. It reads the setup steps for itself, adds ReachAI, and asks you to sign in with your ReachAI account. It also installs a short playbook (`/skill.md`) so it uses ReachAI well.

**Claude and ChatGPT apps** can't add connectors on their own; the agent tells you the clicks. In Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, paste `https://reach-ai-influencer-marketing.vercel.app/api/mcp`, then **Connect** and sign in. No key needed.

### By hand

**Claude Code**

```bash
claude mcp add --transport http reachai https://reach-ai-influencer-marketing.vercel.app/api/mcp
```

Then run `/mcp` in Claude Code, choose **reachai** and sign in.

**Agents that can't sign in** use a personal key: create one on [Agents](/agents) (shown once) and send it as the header `Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY`. Anyone with a key can see and change your campaigns, so keep it private. You can revoke a key at any time and it stops working immediately.

## What your agent can do

| Ask for | What it does |
|---|---|
| Your campaigns | Lists them with status and number of creators found |
| A campaign's details | Shows the brief and where the search is up to |
| A new campaign | Creates one from a short brief. It does **not** start the search |
| What's set up | Your credits and plan, whether Gmail is connected, whether payments are open, and what to do next. Your agent can also hand you the link to connect Gmail or to buy credits: you open it and finish on Google's or the payment page, your agent never pays or agrees for you |
| Your website | Reads your public page as text. Your own agent (not ReachAI's AI) uses it to fill in the brief, asks you for what the page doesn't say (such as the audience country), then creates the campaign. It does **not** start the search |
| A creator search | Your agent writes the search strategy itself, ReachAI checks it (and tells the agent what to fix), then it starts only after your agent has told you the cost in credits and you agreed. Agents can start 3 a day. Your agent can also check your credits |
| Search progress | Tells you whether the search has finished |
| The creators | A ranked table with score, audience, engagement and estimated fair value, with sorting, a minimum score and pages |
| One creator | Full numbers, score breakdown, recent videos and why they fit. For searches your agent started, your agent can also word the "why they fit" points itself: ReachAI checks each one against the facts and sends it back to fix if something isn't supported. After three tries the plain checked facts stay |
| Saved creators | Lists, saves or removes creators on the campaign's shortlist (the same list as the Saved tab) |
| A first message | Your agent writes it in your voice; ReachAI checks it (no price above the creator's fair range, no placeholders, no internal terms) and saves it as a **draft in that creator's chat**. You review and send it in ReachAI |
| Replies and follow-ups | If you switch on **My agent writes replies** in the Autopilot menu, your agent writes the replies and follow-ups with its own AI. It gets the conversation, your voice and the deal's limits, and ReachAI checks each draft (prices only from the deal, never above your limit; nothing invented) and tells your agent what to fix. A passing draft appears in the creator's chat for you to review and send |
| What needs you | Who replied, which drafts are waiting, where each deal stands, and the Track results: spend, views, signups or sales, late posts and who is due payment |

Scores, audience sizes, prices and results are calculated by ReachAI, not by your agent, so they match what you see in the app. The agent only lays them out.

If your agent's client supports slash commands, you also get `/reachai-new-campaign`, `/reachai-draft-pitches` and `/reachai-check-inbox`.

## What it can't do

- Move money, agree a price, close a deal or send a first email. Those stay in ReachAI.
- Send anything on its own. First messages are saved as drafts for you to send. A reply or follow-up your agent wrote can be sent by your agent only if you turned on **Allow my agent to send** for that campaign, and only after you say yes in your chat with it.
- Agree terms, set prices or mark a creator paid. Those stay in the app.
- Send a reply. Your agent can write replies and follow-ups (when you switch it on for a campaign), but they are saved as drafts in the chat: you send them in ReachAI.

## Connecting without copying a key

If your agent can run commands (Claude Code, Codex and similar), it can connect itself with a one-time approval from you:

1. Your agent asks ReachAI for a sign-in code and shows you a short code like `BCDF-GHJK` and a link.
2. You open the link, sign up or in, check the code matches, and press **Approve**.
3. Your agent receives its own key and finishes the setup. You can remove it any time on the **Agents** page.

You approve it yourself: an agent can't approve its own request. A key also works on a short list of read-only ReachAI web endpoints (your campaigns, credits and Gmail status); everything else, such as changing a plan, agreeing a price or sending, is refused for keys.

## Letting your agent send

By default your agent only saves drafts. If you want it to send too, turn on **Allow my agent to send** in the Autopilot menu of that campaign (it works once **My agent writes replies** is on). Then, for a reply or follow-up it wrote:

1. Your agent asks ReachAI for a preview and shows you exactly what would go out: who it goes to, the subject and the full text.
2. You say yes in your chat with the agent.
3. Your agent confirms, and ReachAI sends it from your Gmail the same way the Send button does. The preview is only good for 30 minutes and only for that exact text: if you edit the draft in between, the agent has to ask you again.

Limits: 10 messages a day sent by your agent, only drafts it wrote and ReachAI checked, never a price agreement or a closing (those wait for you in ReachAI), and you get an email and a line in the bell for every message it sends. ReachAI can't see what you tell your agent, so only turn this on for an agent you trust, and keep it out of unattended runs: the scheduled routine never sends.

## Run it on a schedule

Most agents can run a prompt on a schedule. Paste the `reachai-run-routine` prompt (or ask your agent to use the ReachAI routine) into your agent's scheduler. Each run reads your inbox, writes a draft for every conversation waiting for your agent, and finishes with a short summary. A scheduled run never sends anything and never asks questions: it only writes drafts. How you set up a schedule depends on your agent, so follow its own instructions.

If a tool answers with an error, the message says what to do next (for example, wait while a search is running, or reconnect Gmail). Your agent can follow it without you.

## A note on safety

Creator names, bios and summaries come from the internet. ReachAI marks them as untrusted when it hands them to your agent, so a creator can't tell your agent what to do. If your agent also has tools that send email or spend money, keep those on "ask me first".
