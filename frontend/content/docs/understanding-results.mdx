## Final score and deal status

Each creator gets a **final score from 0 to 100**:

| Part | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **Fit** | 55% | How well the creator's content and audience match your brief: topic, audience stage, and whether they talk about your buyers' problems. Judged on what they actually post, not on follower count |
| **Health** | 30% | How trustworthy their numbers look: engagement compared with similar-sized creators, steady views, regular posting |
| **Bonuses** | up to +15 | **Rising** (+10) and **On trend** (+5) |
| **Risk** | minus up to 40 | The biggest of: brand-safety concerns, mostly-ads content, or unusual engagement |

When a channel doesn't list its country and you've chosen target countries, our judge (Jev) reads its bio and posts (language, places, prices, exams, local references) and decides which country its audience is in. If it's clearly one of yours, the creator is treated as being there and the country shows *judged from their posts*. If it's clearly elsewhere, the creator is left out. If there's no real clue, they're kept but ranked a little lower.

From the final score:

| Label | Score | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Strong Buy** | 60+ | Great fit with healthy numbers. Contact first |
| **Consider** | 45–59 | Good option; check their recent videos |
| **Avoid** | under 45 | Weak fit, weak numbers or a real risk |

## Open the creator

Each campaign has three tabs: **Brief**, **Creators** and **[Outreach](/docs/outreach)**. Your results are under **Creators**.

Select a creator to open their panel. It reads top to bottom: **why they fit** your brief, **their posts** (the evidence), **performance numbers**, then **why this score** for the detail. Their **name** opens their channel, **View on YouTube** opens their videos, and clicking their **photo** shows it larger. Social icons are dimmed when we didn't find that account.

At the top, the panel shows how fresh the numbers are (*Numbers from 3 days ago*) and a blue tick when the platform has verified the creator.

### Why this score

For newer results, the panel shows the points behind the score: **brand fit**, **channel health** and any **bonus** (rising or on trend) are added, **risk** is taken away, and the total is the score. When the channel lists no country and its posts give no clear clue, the total is multiplied by 0.8, and the panel says so.

### Posts

The panel sorts the creator's last 12 posts into three tabs. It opens on the first tab that has something in it.

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Best match** | Posts whose title or opening summary mentions your brief's topics, tagged with the topics they match |
| **Above their usual** | Posts with at least 1.5× the creator's usual views |
| **Latest** | Newest first |

Each post shows its thumbnail, views, how it did against the creator's usual (*2.3× usual*), its age, and a **Sponsored** tag when it says it's a paid promotion ("sponsored by", "use code", "#ad"…). Posts under a week old say **New** instead, because they're still collecting views. They're also left out of the creator's usual.

Above the tabs, three tiles sum it up: **On brief** (how many of their last posts are about your brief), **Sponsored** (paid promotions; hover to see the brands) and **Usual views** (a typical post). The views trend next to **Performance metrics** compares their newest posts with the ones before.

Results from before November 4 show the simpler list of recent videos.

## Performance numbers

| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Average views** | Typical views across their recent 15 videos |
| **Reliability** | High, Medium or Low: how steady their views are. Low means a few videos went viral and most didn't |
| **Engagement rate** | Likes and comments as a share of views |
| **Trust score** | How much real conversation their audience has (comments compared with likes) |
| **Forecast** | Expected link clicks per video, based on views and typical click rates for your industry |

## Fair price

The **valuation** is an estimate of what one video from this creator is worth: their typical views × the typical cost per 1,000 views for your industry, adjusted for the creator's country. Use it to judge quotes: a quote far above the fair price is a reason to negotiate or move on.

<Callout type="warn" title="It's an estimate">
  Fair price is a starting point for negotiation, not a quote. Formats (a full dedicated video vs. a short mention), usage rights and exclusivity all change the real price.
</Callout>

## What gets filtered out before you see it

- Channels with **no upload in 180 days**.
- **Suspicious engagement**: under 0.5% engagement across recent videos.
- Channels below the **view floor** for your industry and price range.
- Channels outside your **target countries** or **subscriber range**.

Next to the title, a small chip says how many creators the search checked and how many it kept, for example *347 checked · 31 kept*. Click it to see why the rest were left out.

## Sort, filter and search

- **Sort** by best match (the score), best value (lowest cost per 1,000 views), most views, highest engagement, intent, trust or lowest price.
- **Filter** to rising or on-trend creators, hide unusual engagement, show only saved creators, or narrow by confidence, reliability and price tier. The Filter button shows how many filters are on; **Reset filters** clears them.
- **Search** by creator name or niche.
- Your sort and filters are remembered for each campaign on this browser. Search is not.
- Once a campaign has creators from more than one platform, chips above the list filter by platform. Instagram, X and LinkedIn are coming soon.

### Shortlist
Click the bookmark on a creator to add them to your **shortlist**, and switch between **All** and **Shortlist** at the top of the list. Saving shows up straight away; if it doesn't reach our server, the bookmark goes back and a message says so.

**Start outreach** and **Export** (in the **⋯** menu) act on what you're looking at: the creators you've ticked, otherwise everyone in the current view, for example your shortlist.

## Share and compare

### Compare creators
Select 2 to 4 creators and press **Compare** in the bar at the bottom. You get them side by side: score and its parts, subscribers, typical views, engagement, views trend, posts matching your brief, sponsored posts, country, fair price, cost per 1,000 views and their best recent post. The best value in each row is highlighted.

### Share a shortlist
Press **Share** above the list. A **Sharing** panel opens with three tabs:

- **People:** who has access and how (see below).
- **Activity:** what happened, newest first: who opened the shortlist and when, and every approval, rejection and comment. Click a creator's name to open them. A blue dot on **Share** tells you something new happened since you last looked.
- **By creator:** every creator someone reacted to, with 👍/👎 counts and comments. **Shortlist approved** and **Add approved to deals** act on creators with more 👍 than 👎.

In the list, the Filter menu also has **Approved by reviewers** and **Has feedback**.

Sharing works like Google Drive:

- **Invite people by email** who **Can view** or **Can review**. Each person gets their own link, copied for you when you press **Invite**; send it to them yourself. You see when each link was last opened, and **⋯** lets you show them prices or remove access.
- **General access:** **Restricted** (only people you invite) or **Anyone with the link** (view only, stops working after 30 days).
- **Which creators:** everyone in the view you're in (all creators, or your shortlist if you're looking at it), or only the ones you've selected. A shortlist or selection link shows those creators; ones you add later aren't included.
- **Fair prices are off by default.** Turn them on per person (in **⋯**), for example for an investor, and leave them off for a client.
- **Contact details are never shared** through a link.

People who open a link see the list and each creator's panel, and can compare creators. They can't save, export or start outreach, and the page is hidden from search engines.

### Reviewers
A **Reviewer** can approve (👍) or reject (👎) each creator and leave a comment, still without an account. You see it on your list as a small chip, for example 👍 2 · 👎 1, and in each creator's panel under **Feedback**: who said what, and when. Links for anyone stay view-only, so only people you invited can give feedback. If you remove someone's access, what they already said stays.

## Badges

Some creators carry a badge next to their label. Hover over a badge to see what it means.

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Rising** | Recent videos are doing much better than the creator's usual. Often cheaper now than later |
| **On trend** | Posts about what the niche is talking about right now |
| **Check engagement** | Views, likes or comments look unusual for a channel this size. Ask for audience stats before paying |
| **Country unknown** | The channel doesn't list a country and its posts give no clear clue, so we can't confirm its audience is in your market. These creators are kept but ranked a little lower |

## While a search runs

The progress bar shows which step the search is on, how many creators have been scored so far, and the best one, before the full list is ready.

## Results older than 30 days

YouTube's rules let us keep a channel's numbers for 30 days. Every night we update creators that appear in your results or saved leads, so older campaigns show fresh numbers. If a channel can't be updated (for example it was deleted or made private), its row keeps the name, link and our score and reason, shows **Data expired**, and its numbers are hidden. Run the campaign again to get fresh numbers.

## Why this creator?

Every creator in your list has a one-sentence reason explaining whether their audience is likely to convert for your product, and why.
