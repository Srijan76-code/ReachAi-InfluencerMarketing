## Final score and deal status

Each creator gets a **final score from 0 to 100**. It combines two things:

| Part | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **Fit** | 60% | How well the creator's content and audience match your brief. Judged on what they actually post, not on follower count |
| **Health** | 40% | How trustworthy their numbers look: real conversation in comments, adjusted for how consistent their views are |

From the final score:

| Label | Score | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Strong Buy** | 80+ | Great fit with healthy numbers. Contact first |
| **Consider** | 60–79 | Good option; check their recent videos |
| **Avoid** | under 60 | Weak fit or weak numbers |

## Performance numbers

| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Average views** | Typical views across their recent 15 videos |
| **Reliability** | High, Medium or Low: how steady their views are. Low means a few videos went viral and most didn't |
| **Engagement rate** | Likes and comments as a share of views |
| **Trust score** | How much real conversation their audience has (comments compared with likes) |
| **Forecast** | Expected link clicks per video, based on views and typical click rates for your industry |

## Fair price

The **valuation** is an estimate of what one video from this creator is worth: their typical views × the typical cost per 1,000 views for your industry, adjusted for the creator's country. Use it to judge quotes: a quote far above the fair price is a reason to negotiate or move on.

<Callout type="warn" title="It's an estimate">
  Fair price is a starting point for negotiation, not a quote. Formats (a full dedicated video vs. a short mention), usage rights and exclusivity all change the real price.
</Callout>

## What gets filtered out before you see it

- Channels with **no upload in 180 days**.
- **Suspicious engagement**: under 0.5% engagement across recent videos.
- Channels below the **view floor** for your industry and price range.
- Channels outside your **target countries** or **subscriber range**.

## Why this creator?

Every creator in your list has a one-sentence reason explaining whether their audience is likely to convert for your product, and why.
