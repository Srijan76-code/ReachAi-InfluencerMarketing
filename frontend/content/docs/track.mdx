The **Track** tab shows what your agreed creators' posts brought you. You don't have to do anything: ReachAI checks for new posts and fresh numbers every day, and again when you open Track (or press **Check now**).

## Your goal

At the top: how many signups (or sales, views or clicks) you've got so far, against your goal. Press the pencil to set the goal and, for signups or sales, what one is worth to you. With a value set, ReachAI shows your **return**: what the results are worth next to what you paid creators.

**On pace for ~64 by Dec 1** is a forecast: each live post's views so far, scaled to 30 days by how YouTube videos usually grow (most views come in the first week), with results growing at the same rate. It shows once a post has two days of data and is always marked as an estimate.

The chart shows the last 30 days: your goal's results, views or clicks per day.

## The four numbers

| Number | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Spent** | What you agreed to pay creators, against your budget |
| **Views** | All their posts' views, from YouTube, updated daily |
| **Clicks** | Visits through their tracking links (bots and link previews aren't counted) |
| **Cost per signup** or **Return** | What each result cost you; or, with a value set, what you got back for each dollar |

## Posts

One card per post you agreed: due, late or live.

- **Found by themselves:** every day ReachAI looks at each creator's new uploads. A video whose description has their ReachAI link or code is their post. If none has it, our judge (Jev) reads the new videos' titles and descriptions and says whether one is your sponsored post. Each video is only checked once.
- **Add link**: paste the link yourself if it wasn't found. Its numbers come with the next check.
- **Late** posts turn red, with **Remind them**, which opens their chat with a polite reminder ready to send.
- **What the audience said:** 3 days after a post is live, Jev reads its comments once: the overall feeling, whether people are interested in your product or asking about it, and whether anyone complains about the ad. The most liked comment is shown as written.

## Creators

Ranked best value first, with what each cost, their views (against the views we expected from their channel), clicks, results and cost per result. Each gets a verdict, worked out in code, not by AI:

| Verdict | When |
|---|---|
| **Rebook** | 1.2× or more the views we expected, or cheaper per result than your typical creator |
| **OK** | About what we expected |
| **Skip next time** | Under half the views we expected, or 1.5× or more your typical cost per result |
| **Too early** | Their posts have been live for less than 3 days |

The **⋯** menu adds signups, sales or code uses you counted yourself, or copies their link or code.

## Counting signups and sales

Clicks count by themselves. For signups there are three ways, and you can use any of them:

1. **The snippet on your site.** Open **How results are counted** and copy the two lines: the first goes on every page, the second on the page people see after signing up. A creator's link adds their code to your address (`?ref=SRIJAN42`); the snippet remembers it for 30 days on your site, and when that visitor signs up, ReachAI counts one signup for that creator. Only the creator's code is sent, nothing about the person, and no cookies.
2. **Code uses from your store.** Paste a CSV of code and number of uses (Shopify, Stripe and most stores export this). Codes are matched to the creator who has them.
3. **By hand**, from a creator's **⋯** menu.

We count the last link a person came through, or the code they used. Someone who watched a video and later typed your address in won't be counted.

## To pay

When a creator's post is live (or earlier, if you agreed to pay before they post), they show up under **To pay** with the amount. Pay them the way you agreed, then press **Mark paid** and record the amount, date, how (PayPal, bank transfer, UPI, Wise or other) and a note. ReachAI never moves money. Paid ones fold under **Paid**, with **Undo** if you made a mistake.

## Download your results

**Results** at the top downloads a CSV with one row per creator: price, posts and their links, views, clicks, signups, code uses, cost per result, verdict, their code and link, and when and how you paid. Good for your team, investors or your accountant.
