### Where does the data come from?

From the platforms themselves, at the time you run the campaign. YouTube data comes from the official YouTube Data API, so views and uploads are current, not from a monthly snapshot.

### How accurate is the fit ranking?

Fit is judged from what creators actually post, compared against your brief. In our own tests, ReachAI's picks beat a market-leading tool on the same brief. No ranking is perfect, so review a creator's recent videos before you reach out.

### Why are some creators missing an email?

Emails are only shown when creators publish them (in their channel description or videos). Better contact finding and email verification are on the roadmap.

### How many campaigns can I run?

YouTube limits how many searches we can make each day, so there's a daily cap across all users for now. We're increasing it.

### Which platforms are supported?

YouTube today. X, Instagram and LinkedIn are next: B2B and AI products get YouTube, X and LinkedIn; consumer apps get Instagram and YouTube.
