Connect your Gmail once and ReachAI sends the messages you approve **from your own address**. Creators see a normal email from you, and every message stays in your Gmail's Sent folder.

## Connect
1. Open **Outreach** and press **Connect Gmail**.
2. Sign in with Google and allow both permissions:
   - **Send email:** only the messages you approve.
   - **Read email:** only to read replies in the threads ReachAI started. Nothing else in your inbox is read.
3. You come back to ReachAI, and the button shows your address.

While ReachAI is in early access, Google shows a "Google hasn't verified this app" screen. Press **Advanced**, then **Continue**.

## What happens next
- **Send:** in a creator's chat in Outreach, first messages, suggested replies and your own messages go out with **Send** (or **Send N checked** for several first emails).
- **Replies:** ReachAI checks your creators' email threads when you open Outreach, every minute while ReachAI is open, and every 30 minutes otherwise. A new reply is read by our judge (Jev), and a suggested reply appears in their chat under **Your turn**. You don't paste anything.
- **Their email:** each deal shows **Their email**. It's filled in from their channel when they publish one; otherwise add it yourself.
- **Daily limit:** 40 emails a day, to keep your inbox out of spam folders.

## Landing in the inbox, not spam
A first email from someone the creator has never written to can land in spam, especially between new Gmail accounts. ReachAI sends with your Gmail name and keeps pitches short, plain and personal, which helps. You can help too:
- Set your name in Gmail (**Settings → Accounts → Send mail as**), so creators see who you are.
- Send a few emails a day at first, not dozens.
- Once a creator replies, the rest of the thread reaches their inbox normally.

## Disconnect
Press your address in Outreach, then **Disconnect Gmail**. This removes ReachAI's access at Google too. You can also remove it in your Google account settings.
