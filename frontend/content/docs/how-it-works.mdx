## Cheap work first, expensive work last

Each step narrows the list before a more expensive one runs. AI only writes text for the creators you'll actually contact.

| Who does it | What it does | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Code** | Views, engagement, fair price, forecasts, scores | Numbers must be exact and repeatable |
| **[Jev](https://docs.typesafe.ai/introduction)** | Yes/no, labels and scales: fit, safety, real person | Fast, cheap, and gives real probabilities |
| **Language model** | The strategy, the reasons, the pitches | Only where people read the words |

## Built not to waste your time

- **Platforms run in parallel.** A small test search runs on each extra platform; it only gets a full search if it finds creators who beat your list.
- **A second search** runs when too few creators pass your size and country limits.
- **Runs pick up where they stopped.** If anything fails, the retry continues from the last finished step.
- **Repeat searches are faster.** Briefs, searches, judgments and creator data are remembered.

**Page speed.** Measured with production-shaped data (6,010 campaigns, 18,000 saved leads) and the same benchmark before and after:

| Page data | Before | After | 10 users at once |
|---|---|---|---|
| Campaign list | 151.5 ms | **37.1 ms** | 742 → **153 ms** |
| Creators (leads) | 40.0 ms | **35.0 ms** | 186 → **112 ms** |
| Saved creators | 46.6 ms | **37.4 ms** | 144 → **73 ms** |
| Run status | 53.0 ms | **31.5 ms** | 150 → **107 ms** |

**Quality.** Before the new engine went live, old and new were run on the same 3 briefs and every creator was rated blind (the rater didn't know which engine found it). The new engine never broke your size or country limits; the old one did 5 times.

## Your data

- Creator data comes from the platforms' official sources at search time.
- YouTube numbers are refreshed or removed after 30 days, as YouTube's rules require. Old results then show **Data expired**.
- Nothing an AI writes can send a message or a payment on its own.
