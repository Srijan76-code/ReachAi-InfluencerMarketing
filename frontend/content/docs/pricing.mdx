## How a plan pays for itself

One sponsored post usually costs **$200–2,000**. The two expensive mistakes are paying too much and paying the wrong creator. A worked example (illustrative numbers):

| | Without ReachAI | With ReachAI |
|---|---|---|
| A creator quotes | $800 | $800 |
| You know the fair range | No | **$450–600** (from their real views and your industry) |
| You pay | $800 | ~$600 after negotiating |
| Saved on **one** deal | — | **~$200**, 4× the Starter plan |

The second lever is fit: every creator comes with expected clicks per video, so you can compare **clicks per dollar** across creators before you commit, instead of after.
