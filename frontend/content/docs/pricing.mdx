**Only creator searches use credits.** Outreach, AI drafts, negotiation, Gmail sending, Track and agents are free on every plan.

## Credits

Every account starts on the **Free** plan with **5 welcome credits** plus **3 credits a month**. Your balance is in the top bar; open it for the details and history.

| Search (one platform) | Credits |
|---|---|
| YouTube | 1 |
| X | 3 (coming soon) |
| Instagram | 4 (coming soon) |
| LinkedIn | 8 (coming soon) |

- The cost and your balance show under **Find creators** before you start.
- A search returns up to about 30 ranked creators. **If it finds fewer than 10, the credits come back** automatically.
- If a search fails or you start it again, the credits it held come back.
- Monthly credits reset on the 1st. Welcome credits don't expire.
- Up to 20 searches a day.

## Why it's priced this way

One sponsored post usually costs **$200–2,000**. The expensive mistakes are paying too much and paying the wrong creator. ReachAI shows a fair price range for each creator and expected clicks per video, so you can compare before you commit. Creator databases charge $150–600 a month; ReachAI charges only for searches, which cost us very little, and nothing for the rest.
