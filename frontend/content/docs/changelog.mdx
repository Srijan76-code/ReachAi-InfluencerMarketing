## November 4, 2026

**Fixes**
- Audience location is now sent correctly for every country. Before, the country was read from the first two letters of its name, so "United States" didn't work and "Germany" was searched as Georgia.
- A campaign can't be started with the brand name, product, audience or location missing. Before, empty fields were silently filled with a sample brand.
- Creator languages now follow the country you pick (for example English and Hindi for India), instead of always English and Hindi.
- Reopening a campaign now restores its location and creator size correctly.
- The progress bar keeps showing the current step while a search runs, and **Retry** on a failed search now starts it again.

**Results stay current**
- Every night, creators in your results and saved leads get fresh YouTube numbers. A creator that can't be updated after 30 days shows **Data expired** with its numbers hidden, as YouTube's rules require. See [Understanding your results](/docs/understanding-results#results-older-than-30-days).

**Coming with the new discovery engine**
- Badges on creators (Rising, On trend, Check engagement, Country unknown) and a live count of creators scored while a search runs. See [Understanding your results](/docs/understanding-results).

## November 3, 2026

**Demo and landing page**
- New public [demo campaign](/demo): a real brief, 7 ranked YouTube creators and sample outreach pitches. No sign-in needed.
- Temporary landing page with sign-up, log-in, the demo and these docs.

**Docs**
- New docs at `/docs`, linked from the app's top bar.

**Fixes**
- Fixed the pitch export: Instagram, Twitter and Status columns were empty. LinkedIn is now included.

**Behind the scenes** (rolls out with the next discovery update)
- A new discovery engine, now in testing: platforms are searched in parallel, it searches again when too few creators qualify, interrupted runs pick up where they stopped, and each creator's reason agrees with its label. See [How it works](/docs/how-it-works).
- Backup AI models, so a run can switch provider if one is down.
- Cost tracking for every AI call.
- A shared cache, so repeated searches will be faster and cheaper.

## Earlier
