The **Deals** tab is where you keep track of the creators you're actually working with: what you agreed, when they post, and whether you've paid them.

## Add creators

- From a creator's panel: **Add to deals**.
- From your list: select creators, then **Add N to deals** in the bar at the bottom.
- On the Deals tab: **Add creators**, then pick from your shortlist (or everyone).

New deals start in **Contacted**. If your campaign's goal is signups or sales, the deal type starts as **fee + commission**; for awareness, a **flat fee**.

## The board

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| **Contacted** | You reached out |
| **Negotiating** | Talking price and terms |
| **Agreed** | Price and terms agreed |
| **Content** | They're making it |
| **Live** | The post is up |
| **Paid** | You paid them |

Drag a card to another column, or use its **⋯** menu (also works on a phone). Creators who aren't going ahead go to **Lost**, which is hidden unless you press **Show lost**.

Each card shows the price next to our fair range: **green** within it, **amber** above it, **red** more than twice its top. A card turns red when its post date has passed and it isn't live yet.

The strip at the top adds it up: how many deals, how much you've **committed** against your budget, how many posts are **live**, and how much you've **paid**.

## Fair price for each deal

Every deal shows a **fair price**: what a creator of that size usually earns for this kind of post. It's adjusted for what you're asking for:

| What you ask for | Effect on the fair price |
|---|---|
| Dedicated video | ×1.6 (a sponsored segment is ×1) |
| Short / story | ×0.5 / ×0.35 |
| Using it in your paid ads | +30% to +50% (30 to 90 days) |
| Reposting on your channels | +10% |
| Exclusivity | +20% (30 days) to +50% (a year) |

These are starting values from industry guides; they'll be tuned with real agreed prices.

## Negotiating: the agent

When a creator answers, ReachAI's negotiation agent works out your reply:

1. **It reads their message**: the fee and commission they ask for, whether they're firm or flexible, whether they accept or decline, and any questions they ask. "7 + 20% commission" means $7 plus 20%.
2. **It decides your move**: accept, counter, change the kind of deal (fee + commission, commission only, or their price with a smaller scope), hold your last offer, or walk away. It also picks the commission % and how far to move. It takes everything into account: their usual views, their recent sponsors, your goal and budget, every message so far, and what similar creators agreed on ReachAI.
3. **It writes the message**, answering what they actually said.
4. **It checks itself**: the message may only contain the decided price and commission, and must not promise anything else. If a check fails it rewrites once, then falls back to a short standard message.

The suggestion lands in **To do** with the reasoning, for example "They asked $7 + 20% commission. That's within your limit, so accept it." Nothing is sent until you approve it.

**Your limits always hold.** The agent never offers more than a little above the fair price (15%) or more than your remaining budget. It never offers less than you already offered, and it stops after four rounds with a final offer.

### Start it
- **With Gmail connected**, their email replies come in by themselves and the agent runs on each one.
- **Otherwise**, open their deal and paste their reply (or just type what they asked for, like "$7 + 20% commission") under **Next step**, then press **Suggest a reply**. It takes a few seconds.

### Who does what
| Part | Done by |
|---|---|
| Fair price, your limits, exact amounts | Code |
| Reading their message, choosing the move, deal type, commission and how far to move | Jev, our judge |
| Writing the message | An AI writer, checked by code and Jev |
| Sending it | You, by approving |

A reply that looks like it's trying to instruct an AI is ignored, and the agent tells you to read it yourself.

If the agent can't suggest anything, the deal says why. The usual reason is no fair price, for example a channel with almost no views. Set a fair price range yourself in the deal, then press **Suggest again**.

## To do: the next step for every deal

Switch the Deals tab from **Board** to **To do** to see what to do next on each deal. Board cards with something to do have a small blue dot.

| When | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| They replied | The negotiation agent's reply (offer, accept, or close politely) |
| No answer 4 days after you reached out | **Send a follow-up** (and a polite last one 4 days later, then it stops) |
| The post-by date has passed with no link | **Ask about the late post** (up to 3 times, 3 days apart) |
| The post link is in | **Move to Live** |
| The post is live | **Mark paid** once you've paid them (ReachAI doesn't move money) |

You can:
- **Approve.** With Gmail this **sends** the message; without it, **Copy and mark sent** copies it for you to send.
- **Edit** the message first.
- **Reword with AI** for a different wording with the same terms.
- **Skip** it. A skipped suggestion doesn't come back.
- **Select several** (or Select all), edit any, then **Approve** or **Skip** them together.

Greetings use the creator's first name only when the channel name looks like a person's name ("Hi Krish,"); otherwise they say "Hi there,". Edit it if you know their name.

### With Gmail connected
[Connect Gmail](/docs/gmail) and approving a message **sends** it from your address, in the same email thread. Creators' replies come into their deal by themselves, so you only paste replies that came some other way (a DM or WhatsApp).

## Add a creator you already know
In **Add creators**, paste their YouTube channel link (and their email if you have it) under **Or add by channel link**. ReachAI measures the channel like a search would (usual views, fair price, recent posts) and adds them to your creators and to Deals. They show as **Added by you**, with no fit score.

## Write down what you agreed

Each deal keeps the terms that cause disputes when they aren't written down: **deliverable**, **post-by date**, **deal type** (and commission), **usage rights**, **exclusivity**, whether they **label it as an ad** (#ad or "paid partnership"), and notes.

## Live post and payment

Paste the **link to the live post** (it must be a link on their platform) and move the deal to **Live**. When you've paid, press **Mark paid**: the amount defaults to the agreed price, and you can add a reference (UPI, PayPal, invoice number). ReachAI doesn't move money; you pay creators directly.

Every change is kept on the deal's **timeline**.
