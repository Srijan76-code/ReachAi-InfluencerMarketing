The **Deals** tab is where you work with the creators you're talking to: negotiate in a chat, then track each agreed post until it's paid. It has two views:

- **Negotiating**: an inbox. Creators on the left, the conversation on the right.
- **Content**: one row per agreed creator, from agreed to posted to paid.

## Add creators

- From a creator's panel: **Add to deals**.
- From your list: select creators, then **Add N to deals** in the bar at the bottom.
- Sending a pitch from Outreach adds them by itself.
- On the Deals tab: **Add creators**, then pick from your shortlist (or everyone), or add one by channel link.

If your campaign's goal is signups or sales, the deal type starts as **fee + commission**; for awareness, a **flat fee**.

## Negotiating: an inbox

The list on the left is grouped:

| Group | Means |
|---|---|
| **Your turn** (blue dot) | ReachAI has a reply ready for you |
| **Waiting for them** | You wrote last; it's their move |
| **Agreed** | You agreed; track it in Content |
| **Lost** | Not going ahead (folded away) |

Open a creator to see the whole conversation as a chat: what you sent and what they wrote, from Gmail or pasted. The top line sums it up: fair price, what they ask, what you last offered. The line under it shows the terms; click it to change the deliverable, deal type, commission, usage rights, their email or the fair price.

At the bottom:
- **Write to them**: type any message and press **Send**. With Gmail it goes from your address in the same thread; without it, it's copied for you to send.
- **Paste their reply**: for replies that came by DM or WhatsApp, or just type their ask ("$20 + 10% commission"). ReachAI reads it and suggests a reply.

Use the **⋯** menu to mark a deal agreed or lost, or remove it.

The line at the top adds it up: how many creators you're talking to, how many agreed, how much you've **committed** against your budget, and how much is left **to pay**.

## Fair price for each deal

Every deal shows a **fair price**: what a creator of that size usually earns for this kind of post. It's adjusted for what you're asking for:

| What you ask for | Effect on the fair price |
|---|---|
| Dedicated video | ×1.6 (a sponsored segment is ×1) |
| Short / story | ×0.5 / ×0.35 |
| Using it in your paid ads | +30% to +50% (30 to 90 days) |
| Reposting on your channels | +10% |
| Exclusivity | +20% (30 days) to +50% (a year) |

These are starting values from industry guides; they'll be tuned with real agreed prices.

## Negotiating: the agent

When a creator answers, ReachAI's negotiation agent works out your reply:

1. **It reads their message**: the fee and commission they ask for, whether they're firm or flexible, whether they accept or decline, and any questions they ask. "7 + 20% commission" means $7 plus 20%.
2. **It decides your move**: accept, counter, change the kind of deal (fee + commission, commission only, or their price with a smaller scope), hold your last offer, or walk away. It also picks the commission % and how far to move. It takes everything into account: their usual views, their recent sponsors, your goal and budget, every message so far, and what similar creators agreed on ReachAI.
3. **It writes the message**, answering what they actually said.
4. **It checks itself**: the message may only contain the decided price and commission, and must not promise anything else. If a check fails it rewrites once, then falls back to a short standard message.

The suggestion appears at the bottom of their chat (and they move to **Your turn**) with the reasoning, for example "They asked $7 + 20% commission. That's within your limit, so accept it." Nothing is sent until you approve it.

**Your limits always hold.** The agent never offers more than a little above the fair price (15%) or more than your remaining budget. It never offers less than you already offered, and it stops after four rounds with a final offer.

### Start it
- **With Gmail connected**, their email replies come in by themselves and the agent runs on each one.
- **Otherwise**, open their chat, choose **Paste their reply** at the bottom, and paste it (or just type what they asked for, like "$7 + 20% commission"), then press **Read**. It takes a few seconds.

### Who does what
| Part | Done by |
|---|---|
| Fair price, your limits, exact amounts | Code |
| Reading their message, choosing the move, deal type, commission and how far to move | Jev, our judge |
| Writing the message | An AI writer, checked by code and Jev |
| Sending it | You, by approving |

A reply that looks like it's trying to instruct an AI is ignored, and the agent tells you to read it yourself.

If the agent can't suggest anything, the deal says why. The usual reason is no fair price, for example a channel with almost no views. Set a fair price range yourself right there in the chat, then press **Suggest a reply**.

## Suggestions in the chat

When it's your turn, ReachAI's suggested reply sits at the bottom of the chat with its reasoning ("They asked $15 + 10% commission. Offer $12 + 10%."). You can:
- **Send** it. With Gmail it goes from your address; without it, **Copy** copies it for you.
- **Edit** it first.
- **Reword with AI** for a different wording with the same terms.
- **Skip** it. It doesn't come back.

Writing your own message instead replaces the suggestion; the agent reads your message next time.

| When | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| They replied | The negotiation agent's reply (offer, accept, or close politely) |
| No answer 4 days after you reached out | A follow-up (and a polite last one 4 days later, then it stops) |

Greetings use the creator's first name only when the channel name looks like a person's name ("Hi Krish,"); otherwise they say "Hi there,". Edit it if you know their name.

### With Gmail connected
[Connect Gmail](/docs/gmail) and approving a message **sends** it from your address, in the same email thread. Creators' replies come into their deal by themselves, so you only paste replies that came some other way (a DM or WhatsApp).

## Add a creator you already know
In **Add creators**, paste their YouTube channel link (and their email if you have it) under **Or add by channel link**. ReachAI measures the channel like a search would (usual views, fair price, recent posts) and adds them to your creators and to Deals. They show as **Added by you**, with no fit score.

## Content: from agreed to paid

Each agreed creator is one row with three steps: **Agreed → Posted → Paid**, and one button for what's next:

1. **Add post link** when their post is up (it must be a link on their platform). Set the **post by** date so ReachAI can tell you when it's late; late rows turn red with **Send a reminder**.
2. **Mark paid** once you've paid them. ReachAI doesn't move money; you pay creators directly.

The **⋯** menu opens the conversation, undoes the last step, or removes the deal.
