The **Deals** tab is where you keep track of the creators you're actually working with: what you agreed, when they post, and whether you've paid them.

## Add creators

- From a creator's panel: **Add to deals**.
- From your list: select creators, then **Add N to deals** in the bar at the bottom.
- On the Deals tab: **Add creators**, then pick from your shortlist (or everyone).

New deals start in **Contacted**. If your campaign's goal is signups or sales, the deal type starts as **fee + commission**; for awareness, a **flat fee**.

## The board

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| **Contacted** | You reached out |
| **Negotiating** | Talking price and terms |
| **Agreed** | Price and terms agreed |
| **Content** | They're making it |
| **Live** | The post is up |
| **Paid** | You paid them |

Drag a card to another column, or use its **⋯** menu (also works on a phone). Creators who aren't going ahead go to **Lost**, which is hidden unless you press **Show lost**.

Each card shows the price next to our fair range: **green** within it, **amber** above it, **red** more than twice its top. A card turns red when its post date has passed and it isn't live yet.

The strip at the top adds it up: how many deals, how much you've **committed** against your budget, how many posts are **live**, and how much you've **paid**.

## Negotiate a fair price

Open a deal and type **Their quote** under **Price**, then press **Save** (or Enter). ReachAI tells you whether it's fair, above fair or far too high, and suggests the next step. The fair price is adjusted for what you're asking for:

| What you ask for | Effect on the fair price |
|---|---|
| Dedicated video | ×1.6 (a sponsored segment is ×1) |
| Short / story | ×0.5 / ×0.35 |
| Using it in your paid ads | +30% to +50% (30 to 90 days) |
| Reposting on your channels | +10% |
| Exclusivity | +20% (30 days) to +50% (a year) |

These are starting values from industry guides; they'll be tuned with real agreed prices.

| Verdict | When |
|---|---|
| **Good price** | Below the fair range |
| **Fair, accept** | Within the fair range |
| **Counter** at $X | Above the range: offer the middle of it |
| **Too high** | More than 2× the top: walk away, or offer the top at most |

## Needs you: the next step for every deal

Switch the Deals tab from **Board** to **To do** to see what to do next on each deal. Cards on the board with something to do have a small blue dot. ReachAI suggests each step; **nothing is sent or changed until you approve it**.

| When | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| No answer 4 days after you reached out | **Send a follow-up** (and a polite last one 4 days later, then it stops) |
| Their reply states one price | **Write down their quote** |
| Their quote is above the fair price | **Counter** with a ready message (your budget, a lower fee + commission, or their price with a smaller scope) |
| Their quote is fair | **Accept and agree** |
| They accept the offer you sent | **Agree** at the price you offered |
| They turn it down | **Mark as lost** |
| The post-by date has passed with no link | **Ask about the late post** (up to 3 times, 3 days apart) |
| The post link is in | **Move to Live** |
| The post is live | **Mark paid** once you've paid them (ReachAI doesn't move money) |

Each suggestion says why. You can:
- **Approve**. For messages this is **Copy and mark sent**: the message is copied so you can paste it into your email or DMs, and it's saved on the deal's timeline.
- **Edit** the message first.
- **Reword with AI** for counters, to make the wording friendlier.
- **Skip** it. A skipped suggestion doesn't come back.
- **Select several** (or Select all), edit any of them, then **Approve** or **Skip** them together. Your edits are what gets approved.

### Paste their reply
When a creator answers, open their deal and paste the reply under **Next step**, then press **Read reply**. Our judge (Jev) reads it (do they give a price, accept, decline, want commission, say the price is firm?) and the next step updates. If you're in Contacted or Negotiating and nothing is suggested yet, this is where to start.

### Negotiating over several rounds
ReachAI remembers what you offered and what they asked. The deal panel lists it ("You: Offered $180 · They: Asked $260").
- The first counter is the middle of the fair range.
- If they come back higher, the next offer moves part of the way toward their price. It's never less than you already offered, and never above the top of the fair range.
- At the top of the range, or after three rounds, it becomes your **final offer**. If they're still above it, ReachAI suggests marking the deal lost.
- Their reply is read in light of your last offer, so "sounds good" means agreeing to *that* price.

Counters pick one of three approaches:
- **Counter with your budget**
- **Lower fee + commission** (also the default when your goal is signups or sales)
- **Their price, smaller scope** (no paid-ads use or exclusivity)

### Who does what
| Part | Done by |
|---|---|
| Fair price, verdict, counter amount, every number in a message | Code (worked out from their usual views and the deal terms) |
| Reading their reply | Jev, our judge |
| Which next step to suggest | Code, from the deal and what Jev read |
| Wording the message | A template, or AI if you press **Reword with AI**; code checks the numbers didn't change, otherwise you keep the template |
| Doing it | You, by approving |

Their reply is never sent to the AI writer, and a reply that looks like it's trying to instruct an AI is ignored.

Greetings use the creator's first name only when the channel name looks like a person's name ("Hi Krish,"); otherwise they say "Hi there,". Edit it if you know their name.

## Write down what you agreed

Each deal keeps the terms that cause disputes when they aren't written down: **deliverable**, **post-by date**, **deal type** (and commission), **usage rights**, **exclusivity**, whether they **label it as an ad** (#ad or "paid partnership"), and notes.

## Live post and payment

Paste the **link to the live post** (it must be a link on their platform) and move the deal to **Live**. When you've paid, press **Mark paid**: the amount defaults to the agreed price, and you can add a reference (UPI, PayPal, invoice number). ReachAI doesn't move money; you pay creators directly.

Every change is kept on the deal's **timeline**.
